# Dispute Resolution — When LHDN Comes Knocking

Navigate the full lifecycle from LHDN query letter to Special Commissioners appeal. Covers additional assessments, penalties, voluntary disclosure, settlement negotiation, and the decision framework for when to fight vs concede.

---

## The Dispute Timeline

```
LHDN issues query letter / audit notification
  │
  ├─→ Respond within 30 days (or timeframe stated)
  │
  ▼
LHDN proposes adjustment (audit findings letter)
  │
  ├─→ Negotiate / provide additional information
  │
  ▼
LHDN issues ADDITIONAL ASSESSMENT [s.90(2)] — Form JA
  │  (includes: additional tax + penalty)
  │
  ├─→ PAY: tax due within 30 days of Form JA
  │         (payment does NOT waive right to appeal)
  │
  ├─→ APPEAL: File Form Q within 30 days of Form JA
  │
  ▼
Form Q filed — Notice of Appeal to Special Commissioners
  │
  ├─→ Settlement discussions with LHDN (can settle at any point)
  │
  ▼
Special Commissioners hearing
  │
  ├─→ SC decision
  │
  ▼
High Court appeal (on questions of LAW only, not fact)
  │
  ├─→ Court of Appeal → Federal Court (rare)
  │
  ▼
Final resolution
```

## Types of LHDN Action

### Desk audit (s.90/s.91)

```
TRIGGER: LHDN reviews filed return, identifies discrepancy
SCOPE: Specific items queried (not full-scope audit)
RESPONSE: Provide documentation/explanation for queried items
TIMELINE: Usually 1-3 items, resolved within 3-6 months
RISK LEVEL: Low-medium (isolated adjustments)
```

### Field audit (s.90/s.91)

```
TRIGGER: Risk-based selection (GP margin, industry benchmark, refund, tip-off)
SCOPE: Full examination of books, records, documents for selected YA(s)
RESPONSE: Full cooperation required. Provide all records.
TIMELINE: 6-18 months (complex audits: 2-3 years)
RISK LEVEL: High (multiple adjustments likely, penalties compounded)

AUDIT SCOPE MAY EXPAND:
  - Initially selected for YA 2023, but if issues found → extended to YA 2021-2024
  - LHDN can audit up to 5 years back from current YA (with exceptions)
```

### Investigation (s.90(3) — fraud/wilful default)

```
TRIGGER: Suspected fraud, wilful default, or negligence
SCOPE: Unlimited — can go back to ANY year of assessment
TIMELINE: 1-3 years
RISK LEVEL: Critical (criminal prosecution possible, penalties up to 300%)
DISTINCTION: Regular audit = 5-year limit. Investigation = NO time limit.
```

## Penalty Framework

### Rates (s.113 and related)

| Offence | Penalty Range | Typical Range |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Incorrect return (s.113(1)) | 100% of tax undercharged (equal penalty) | 100% |
| Incorrect return (s.113(2) — wilful) | 100%-300% of tax undercharged | 200-300% |
| Failure to furnish return (s.112(3)) | RM200-RM20,000 + 300% of tax | Varies |
| Late filing (s.112(3) — composited) | Varies | RM200-RM20,000 |
| Late payment (s.103(3)/(8)) | 10% increase on unpaid tax | 10% flat |
| s.114 — Wilful evasion (criminal) | Fine RM1,000-RM20,000 / imprisonment ≤ 3 years / both + 300% | Prosecution |

### Penalty negotiation in practice

```
LHDN'S INTERNAL PENALTY MATRIX (based on Operational Guidelines):

VOLUNTARY DISCLOSURE BEFORE AUDIT:
  Technical adjustment (no intent): 15% - 35% penalty
  Negligence: 35% - 45% penalty
  
DURING AUDIT (cooperative):
  Technical: 45% penalty
  Negligence: 45% - 60% penalty
  Wilful default: 60% - 100% penalty

DURING AUDIT (non-cooperative):
  Technical: 60% penalty
  Negligence: 80% - 100% penalty
  Wilful default: 100% - 300% penalty

AFTER ASSESSMENT / APPEAL:
  Settled at SC stage: typically 60-100% (depends on strength of case)
  SC decision against taxpayer: 100% (no reduction)
```

## Form Q — Notice of Appeal

### Requirements

```
MUST BE FILED WITHIN 30 DAYS of Form JA (additional assessment)

CONTENTS (prescribed form):
  □ Appellant's name, address, TIN
  □ YA of assessment
  □ Date of Form JA
  □ Amount of additional assessment disputed (partial or full)
  □ Grounds of appeal (specific, not generic)
  □ Facts supporting the grounds
  □ Issues of law raised

CRITICAL:
  - 30 days is ABSOLUTE. No extension. Miss it = assessment becomes FINAL.
  - Filing Form Q does NOT stay payment. Tax must still be paid.
  - But: can apply to DG for stay of payment pending appeal [s.103(2)]
```

### Grounds of appeal — what works

```
STRONG GROUNDS:
  □ Misapplication of law (wrong section cited, wrong interpretation)
  □ Facts not considered (documents provided but ignored)
  □ Failure to follow Public Ruling (LHDN bound by its own PRs)
  □ Precedent from prior SC/court decisions
  □ Incorrect valuation (backed by independent expert)
  □ Penalty disproportionate to offence

WEAK GROUNDS:
  × "We disagree with LHDN's view" (without legal basis)
  × "Other companies do it this way" (not a legal argument)
  × "Our accountant told us it was correct" (reliance on advisor not a defence)
  × "We've always done it this way" (custom doesn't override statute)
```

## Settlement vs Litigation Decision Framework

```
FACTORS TO WEIGH:

┌─────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Factor                      │ Assessment                                │
├─────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Strength of legal position  │ [Strong / Moderate / Weak]                │
│ Quantum at stake            │ RM [amount] tax + RM [amount] penalty     │
│ Precedent (prior SC cases)  │ [Favourable / Mixed / Against us]         │
│ Documentation quality       │ [Complete / Partial / Weak]               │
│ LHDN officer's position     │ [Firm / Open to negotiation]              │
│ Cost of litigation           │ RM [estimated legal + expert fees]        │
│ Time to resolution          │ [months at SC vs months to settle]        │
│ Client appetite for fight   │ [Will fight / Wants certainty]            │
│ Reputational risk           │ [Public hearing? Named in reports?]       │
│ Relationship preservation   │ [Ongoing business vs one-off]             │
└─────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┘

DECISION RULES:
  IF legal position STRONG + documentation COMPLETE + quantum > RM100k:
    → FIGHT. File Form Q. Proceed to SC.
    
  IF legal position MODERATE + quantum < RM50k:
    → SETTLE. Negotiate penalty reduction. Save costs.
    
  IF legal position WEAK + documentation WEAK:
    → CONCEDE. Pay and move on. Fighting wastes money and goodwill.
    
  IF quantum > RM500k regardless of strength:
    → ALWAYS file Form Q (preserve rights). Then evaluate settlement.
```

## Voluntary Disclosure

### When to disclose proactively

```
IF you discover an error in a filed return:
  - And LHDN has NOT yet queried it
  - You CAN file voluntary disclosure for reduced penalty

MECHANISM: s.77B (amended return)
  - File revised return with correct figures
  - Pay additional tax immediately
  - LHDN typically imposes 15-35% penalty (vs 100%+ if caught in audit)
  
SAVINGS: On RM100k additional tax:
  - Voluntary disclosure: RM15k-35k penalty
  - Caught in audit: RM45k-100k penalty
  - Caught in investigation: RM100k-300k penalty

WHEN TO DISCLOSE:
  □ Material error discovered (wrong rate applied, income omitted, CA overclaimed)
  □ New information received (e.g., learn that an expense was actually capital)
  □ Prior agent's computation found to be wrong
  □ Client confesses to undisclosed income
  □ You suspect LHDN already has the information (e.g., from counterparty's records)
```

### When NOT to disclose

```
  × Trivial amounts (RM < 5,000 additional tax) — risk of triggering broader review
  × Position is genuinely debatable (grey area, not error) — stand by your position
  × You've been notified of audit (too late for voluntary disclosure benefit)
  × The "error" benefits the client (overpaid tax — just file amended return for refund)
```

## Amended Returns [s.77B]

```
TIME LIMIT: Within 5 years from date return was originally due (not filed)

EFFECT:
  - Additional tax: assessed and penalty imposed (reduced rate for voluntary)
  - Tax overpaid: refund (no penalty, but LHDN will review the claim)

PROCESS:
  1. Prepare revised computation showing correct position
  2. Compute additional tax or refund
  3. Submit via e-Filing or to LHDN branch
  4. Pay additional tax (if any) with submission
  5. Wait for LHDN to review and issue revised assessment
  
CAUTION:
  - Amended return opens the ENTIRE return to review (not just the corrected item)
  - LHDN may discover other issues while reviewing your amended return
  - Weigh: is the correction worth the exposure of re-opening the file?
```

## The "Takeover" Workflow (New Client from Prior Agent)

```
PHASE 1 — RETRIEVE AND REVIEW (first 2 weeks):
  □ Obtain copies of last 3 years' computations from prior agent
  □ Obtain Form C / BE copies (as filed)
  □ Obtain any LHDN correspondence (queries, Form JA, Form Q)
  □ Obtain fixed asset register and CA schedule
  □ Obtain loss memorandum (if losses exist)
  □ Obtain CP204 estimate history
  □ Obtain TP documentation (if applicable)

PHASE 2 — VERIFY CARRY-FORWARDS (critical):
  □ Losses: verify year of origin, quantum, and remaining years
  □ Losses: check shareholder continuity since loss arose
  □ CA: verify residual by asset, check for disposed assets still being claimed
  □ CA: verify rates used are correct per asset category
  □ Incentives: verify remaining years and conditions still met
  □ Donations/zakat: check carried-forward amounts (if any)

PHASE 3 — IDENTIFY ERRORS IN PRIOR COMPUTATIONS:
  □ Are all add-backs correctly identified? (common miss: depreciation in COGS)
  □ Are all deductions properly claimed? (common miss: double deductions)
  □ Is CA schedule complete? (common miss: assets omitted, wrong rates)
  □ Is SME status correctly determined? (common miss: not checking all 5 conditions)
  □ Were WHT obligations met? (common miss: non-resident payments not caught)
  □ Was entertainment correctly apportioned? (common miss: blanket 50%)

PHASE 4 — QUANTIFY EXPOSURE AND OPPORTUNITY:
  □ Errors against client (underpaid tax): quantify exposure, consider voluntary disclosure
  □ Errors for client (overpaid tax): quantify refund potential via s.77B amended return
  □ Statute limitation: can we still amend? (5-year window from original due date)
  □ Recommend actions: file amended returns? disclose? or leave alone?

PHASE 5 — TRANSITION:
  □ File Form CP600 (change of tax agent) with LHDN
  □ Ensure prior agent provides all original documents
  □ Set up proper file structure for ongoing engagement
  □ Brief client on findings and recommended actions
```

## LHDN Query Response — Best Practices

```
WHEN RESPONDING TO LHDN QUERIES:

DO:
  □ Respond within stated timeframe (usually 30 days)
  □ Answer ONLY what is asked (do not volunteer additional information)
  □ Provide supporting documents for every figure queried
  □ Reference specific PRs and sections that support your position
  □ Keep copies of everything submitted
  □ Send via registered mail or e-Filing (proof of submission)
  □ Request extension in writing if needed (before deadline)
  □ Maintain professional, factual tone

DON'T:
  × Ignore the letter (automatic adverse assessment)
  × Provide documents for items NOT queried (opens new lines of enquiry)
  × Make admissions or concessions without quantifying the impact
  × Promise to "look into it" without providing substantive response
  × Send original documents (copies only, keep originals)
  × Call the officer to "explain" without written follow-up (verbal = no record)
```

## NEVER

- Let the 30-day Form Q deadline pass without filing (even if you plan to settle)
- Advise client not to pay the assessed tax (payment does not waive appeal rights)
- Volunteer information that wasn't requested in a query letter
- Assume LHDN's position is always correct (challenge with legal basis)
- Assume LHDN's position is always wrong (know when to concede)
- Ignore the cost-benefit of litigation (RM20k dispute with RM30k legal costs = settle)
- Forget that amended returns open the entire return to re-examination
