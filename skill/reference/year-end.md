# Year-End Planning — Tactical Pre-FYE Actions

Time-sensitive planning in the final 1-3 months before financial year end. Not structural strategy (that's `tax-strategy.md`) — this is operational: "What can we still do before the books close?"

---

## When to Use

```
TRIGGER: Client's FYE is within 90 days (3 months)
PURPOSE: Maximise deductions, manage cash tax, avoid penalties
CONSTRAINT: Only actions that can be EXECUTED before FYE (not just planned)
```

### Step 2: Identify the gap

```
SCENARIOS:

A. Tax tracking ABOVE estimate (underestimation risk):
   → Consider: 9th month CP204 revision (increase only)
   → Consider: accelerating deductions to reduce actual tax
   → Quantify: penalty exposure = 10% × (shortfall beyond 30%)

B. Tax tracking BELOW estimate (overestimation — no penalty, but cash flow drag):
   → Cash locked in monthly instalments that won't be refunded until Form C filed
   → If 6th month window still open: DECREASE estimate
   → If past 6th month: no action available (9th month can only increase)

C. Tax tracking AT ESTIMATE:
   → Focus on optimisation: can we reduce tax further before FYE?
   → Each RM10k reduction at 24% rate = RM2,400 cash saving
```

## Year-End Action Levers

### Lever 1: Bonus Declarations

```
TAX EFFECT: Deduction in company; income in employee's hands

TIMING RULE:
  - Bonus is deductible when LIABILITY to pay crystallises (board resolution date)
  - Board resolution BEFORE FYE = deduction in current year
  - Board resolution AFTER FYE = deduction in NEXT year
  - Payment must follow within reasonable time (2-3 months after FYE accepted by LHDN)

COMPUTATION:
  Bonus amount:                                   RM XXX
  Company tax saving (at 24%):                    RM XXX
  Employee tax cost (at marginal rate):           RM XXX
  Employer EPF cost (12-13%):                     RM XXX
  Net group benefit:                              RM XXX

REQUIREMENT: Board minutes dated BEFORE FYE. Specific amount. Named recipients.
             Generic "performance bonus to be determined" = NOT deductible this year.
```

### Lever 2: Asset Purchases (CA)

```
TIMING RULE:
  - CA (IA) given in year asset FIRST USED — not purchased, not delivered, USED.
  - Asset purchased 15 December but installed/commissioned 5 January = CA in NEXT year.
  - For Budget 2026 ACA: QCE must be INCURRED before 31 Dec 2026 (purchase/delivery).

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Identify planned Q1 purchases that can be pulled forward to current month
  □ Confirm asset will be IN USE before FYE (not just ordered/paid)
  □ Check: does ACA (20% IA / 40% AA) apply? Window closes 31 Dec 2026.
  □ Check: does Automation CA (200%) apply? Window closes YA 2027.
  □ Check: small value assets — anything < RM2,000 that can be purchased separately?

QUANTIFY:
  Asset cost (QE):                                RM XXX
  Year 1 CA (IA + AA):                            RM XXX
  Tax saving this year:                           RM XXX
  vs. if purchased next year (Year 1 CA):         RM XXX
  ACCELERATION BENEFIT:                           RM XXX (NPV of timing difference)
```

### Lever 3: Bad Debt Write-Off

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Deductible in year of WRITE-OFF (not year debt became overdue)
  - Board resolution + accounting entry + evidence = crystallises deduction

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Review aged debtors: which are > 12 months overdue?
  □ For each candidate: has recovery action been taken? (demand letters, legal)
  □ If recovery hopeless: prepare board resolution to write off BEFORE FYE
  □ Evidence needed: demand letters sent, debtor tracing, insolvency notice, or
    amount too small to justify legal cost [PR 04/2019 para 5.3.2]

QUANTIFY:
  Total bad debts to write off:                   RM XXX
  Tax saving (at 24%):                            RM XXX
  
CAUTION: General provision (e.g., ECL model % of debtors) = NOT deductible.
         Must be SPECIFIC debts, individually identified.
```

### Lever 4: Stock Write-Off / Obsolescence

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Deductible when stock is written off or written down per PR 02/2020
  - Requires: physical verification, board approval, evidence of obsolescence/damage

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Conduct physical stock take before FYE
  □ Identify obsolete/damaged/expired stock
  □ Board resolution approving write-off (with quantum)
  □ Physical destruction or disposal (keep evidence/photos)
  □ Ensure write-off is in current year's accounts (not next year)

QUANTIFY:
  Stock written off:                              RM XXX
  Tax saving (at 24%):                            RM XXX
```

### Lever 5: Repairs and Maintenance

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Revenue expense deductible when INCURRED (work done and invoiced)
  - Work completed before FYE + invoiced before FYE = current year deduction
  - Work completed before FYE but invoiced after FYE = still current year (accrual basis)

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Planned maintenance for Q1 next year: can it be done in December instead?
  □ Overdue repairs: complete before FYE for current year deduction
  □ Check: is it genuinely REPAIR (revenue) or IMPROVEMENT (capital)?
    - Like-for-like replacement = revenue
    - Upgrade/enhancement = capital (add to FA register, claim CA)

QUANTIFY:
  Brought-forward repairs:                        RM XXX
  Tax saving (at 24%):                            RM XXX
```

### Lever 6: Donations

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Deductible when PAID (not pledged or promised)
  - Cap: 10% of aggregate income — excess permanently lost

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Calculate projected aggregate income
  □ Calculate 10% cap
  □ Current donations YTD: RM [X] — headroom remaining: RM [Y]
  □ If headroom exists: make additional donations before FYE
  □ If headroom is zero: DO NOT over-donate (excess = permanently wasted)
  □ Ensure donations are to s.44(6) APPROVED bodies (get receipt before FYE)

QUANTIFY:
  Available headroom:                             RM XXX
  Tax saving if maximised (at 24%):               RM XXX
```

### Lever 7: Training and Double Deductions

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Double deduction when expenditure INCURRED (training completed and invoiced)
  - For training: course must be completed in the basis period
  - Approval/certification must be from approved body

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Any planned Q1 training that can be completed before FYE?
  □ Training at approved institutions = 200% deduction
  □ Other qualifying double deductions: export promotion, disabled employees
  □ Each RM10k of qualifying spend = RM20k deduction = RM4,800 tax saving

QUANTIFY:
  Eligible training expenditure:                  RM XXX
  Double deduction benefit:                       RM XXX
  Tax saving (at 24%):                            RM XXX
```

### Lever 8: Zakat Payment

```
TIMING RULE:
  - Deductible when PAID to approved state Islamic authority
  - Cap: 2.5% of aggregate income (SEPARATE from donation cap)

PRE-FYE ACTIONS:
  □ Calculate 2.5% of projected aggregate income
  □ Has company zakat been paid this year?
  □ If not: pay before FYE for current year deduction
  □ Ensure payment to APPROVED state Islamic body (get official receipt)

QUANTIFY:
  2.5% cap amount:                                RM XXX
  Tax saving if paid (at 24%):                    RM XXX
```

### Lever 9: CP204 Revision

```
IF within 6th month of basis period:
  → Can INCREASE or DECREASE estimate
  → Decreasing: reduces cash outflow for remaining instalments
  → Increasing: reduces underestimation penalty risk

IF within 9th month of basis period:
  → Can INCREASE only
  → If actual is tracking above estimate by > 30%: REVISE UPWARD
  → Penalty avoided: 10% × (amount above 30% threshold)

IF past 9th month:
  → No revision possible
  → Consider other levers to REDUCE actual tax (bringing actual closer to estimate)
```

## Decision Matrix: Do vs Defer

```
┌──────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┐
│ Situation                │ DO before FYE              │ DEFER to next year       │
├──────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┤
│ Company is profitable    │ Accelerate deductions      │ Defer income if possible │
│ Company is in loss       │ Accelerate income          │ Defer deductions         │
│ Losses expiring next YA  │ Create income to absorb    │ —                        │
│ ACA window closing       │ Buy qualifying assets NOW  │ —                        │
│ CP204 over-estimated     │ — (can't increase income)  │ Declare bonus next year  │
│ CP204 under-estimated    │ Increase deductions        │ —                        │
│ Near SME CI threshold    │ Add deductions to stay ≤600k│ Defer income            │
│ Near donation cap        │ Make remaining donations   │ —                        │
└──────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┘
```

## NEVER

- Recommend actions that cannot physically be completed before FYE (be realistic on timeline)
- Suggest backdating board resolutions or invoices (that's fraud, not planning)
- Ignore the CP204 position (penalty risk is real money)
- Recommend over-donating beyond the 10% cap (excess is wasted permanently)
- Forget that asset purchase timing depends on "in use" date, not purchase date, for CA
- Present year-end planning without a BEFORE and AFTER tax comparison
- Assume client has unlimited cash — each lever requires actual expenditure
- Recommend creating artificial losses through sham transactions (s.140 anti-avoidance)
