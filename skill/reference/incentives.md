# Tax Incentives

**Legislative basis:** ITA 1967; Promotion of Investments Act 1986 (PIA 1986); various P.U.(A) orders
**Administering bodies:** MIDA, MOSTI, LHDN
**Last updated:** Includes Budget 2026 measures (tabled 10 October 2025)

---

## 1. Pioneer Status (PS) / Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)

**NOTE:** A New Investment Incentive Framework is replacing PS/ITA. Pilot phase ended 2025. Full implementation: manufacturing sector Q1 2026, services sector Q2 2026. The framework below remains applicable for existing approvals and any transition period.

### Pioneer Status — Standard
- Income tax exemption on **70% of statutory income** for **5 years**
- Remaining 30% taxed at prevailing CIT rate (24%)

### Pioneer Status — Enhanced (National/Strategic Importance)
- **100% of SI**, 5+5 years (up to 10 years total)

### Pioneer Status — Enhanced (High-Tech)
- **100% of SI**, 5 years

### Pioneer Status — Small-Scale Companies
- **100% of SI**, 5 years

### Loss/CA Treatment
- Unabsorbed pioneer losses and capital allowances from the pioneer period can be carried forward post-pioneer
- Pioneer period losses are ring-fenced during pioneer period

### Investment Tax Allowance — Standard
- **60% of QCE** incurred over 5 years
- Utilised against **70% of SI**
- Balance 30% taxed at prevailing rate

### ITA — Enhanced (National/Strategic Importance)
- **100% QCE** against **100% SI**, 5 years

### ITA — Enhanced (High-Tech)
- **60% QCE** against **100% SI**, 5 years

### ITA — Small-Scale Companies
- **60% QCE** against **100% SI**, 5 years

### ITA Carry Forward
- Unutilised ITA can be carried forward indefinitely (subject to conditions)

## 2. Reinvestment Allowance (RA) — Schedule 7A

### Eligibility
- Resident companies in operation for ≥ **36 months**
- Must incur QCE to **expand, modernise, automate, or diversify** existing manufacturing or approved agricultural project

### Allowance
- **60% of QCE**, utilised against **70% of SI**
- Available for **15 consecutive years** from first year of claim

### Special Rules
- 70% restriction does NOT apply if project achieves prescribed productivity levels
- Asset must not be disposed within **5 years** or RA is withdrawn

## 3. NIMP 2030 Reinvestment Incentives (Post-RA)

For companies that have exhausted their 15-year RA period:

| Tier | Allowance | Period |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Tier 1 | ITA of 100% QCE against 100% SI | 5 years |
| Tier 2 | ITA of 60% QCE against 70% SI | 5 years |

Application window: 1 January 2024 to **31 December 2028**.

## 4. Automation Capital Allowance (IR 4.0)

- ACA of **200%** on QCE up to **RM10 million**
- For companies in manufacturing/services operating ≥ 36 months
- Equipment must incorporate at least one IR 4.0 element
- **Effective: YA 2023 to YA 2027** (no extension beyond 2027 announced in Budget 2026)

## 5. Budget 2026 ACA — Plant, Machinery, ICT (NEW)

Full write-off within ~2 years (20% IA + 40% AA) for:
- Heavy machinery, plant, general machinery acquired from **local manufacturers**
- ICT equipment and computer software
- Consultation, licensing, and incidental fees for software development/customisation

**Effective:** QCE incurred **11 October 2025 to 31 December 2026**

### e-Invoice ACA (Gazetted April 2026)
- ACA for ICT equipment purchased for e-Invoice implementation
- ACA for software customisation costs for e-Invoice
- Fully enacted via gazette orders

## 7. Global Services Hub (GSH)

- New company: CIT rate of **5% or 10%** for up to 10 years
- Existing company: CIT rate on value-added income at 5% or 10% for 5 years
- Non-citizen C-Suite (salary ≥ RM35,000/month): 15% PIT for 3 years (max 3 individuals)
- Applications by **31 December 2027**
- Enhanced rates within JS-SEZ (5% for up to 15-20 years)

- Effective date: TBA

## 15. Key Constraints

### Mutual Exclusivity
- Companies generally must choose between PS and ITA (cannot claim both simultaneously)
- Companies currently enjoying PS, ITA, or RA are excluded from Group Relief (Section 44A)

### Application Required
- Most incentives require formal application to MIDA or relevant agency
- Approval is discretionary and conditional

### Compliance Requirements
- Must meet conditions throughout incentive period
- Failure to comply → incentive withdrawn, tax recalculated at standard rates

## 16. LLP Profit Distribution Tax (NEW YA 2026)

- **2% tax** on chargeable income from profit distributions from LLPs exceeding **RM100,000 per annum**
- Applies to both resident and non-resident individual partners
- Purpose: align LLP income with company dividend tax treatment
- Effective: YA 2026

---

## 17. Foreign-Sourced Income (FSI) Exemption

- Tax exemption on foreign-sourced dividends and capital gains for resident companies and LLPs: **extended 4 years** (1 Jan 2027 - 31 Dec 2030)
- Scope expanded to include **cooperative societies and trust bodies**
- (Prior to this, transitional 3% concessionary rate applied for certain FSI)
