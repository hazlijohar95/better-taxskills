# Stamp Duty — Instruments, Rates, Reliefs, and Penalties

Compute stamp duty on instruments of transfer, loan agreements, share transfers, and corporate restructuring. Governed by the Stamp Act 1949 (SA), completely separate from ITA 1967.

---

## Scope of Charge

```
STAMP DUTY IS CHARGED ON INSTRUMENTS (documents), not transactions.
If there is no instrument, there is no stamp duty.
If the instrument is not executed in Malaysia and does not relate to
Malaysian property/shares, there is no stamp duty.

CHARGEABLE INSTRUMENTS [First Schedule, SA 1949]:
  - Instruments of transfer (property, shares, business)
  - Loan/financing agreements (conventional and Islamic)
  - Lease/tenancy agreements
  - Service agreements
  - Partnership agreements
  - Insurance policies
  - Powers of attorney
```

---

## Rates — Property Transfers (MOT / Memorandum of Transfer)

### Ad valorem rates (effective 1 January 2025)

| Property value (consideration or MV, whichever higher) | Rate |
|-------------------------------------------------------|------|
| First RM100,000 | 1% |
| RM100,001 to RM500,000 | 2% |
| RM500,001 to RM1,000,000 | 3% |
| RM1,000,001 to RM10,000,000 | 4% |
| Above RM10,000,000 | 4% (capped) |

### Computation example

```
Property value: RM2,000,000

First RM100,000 × 1%      = RM  1,000
Next RM400,000 × 2%       = RM  8,000
Next RM500,000 × 3%       = RM 15,000
Next RM1,000,000 × 4%     = RM 40,000
                    TOTAL  = RM 64,000
```

## Rates — Share Transfers

### Listed shares (transferred on Bursa Malaysia)

```
Rate: RM1.50 per RM1,000 or fraction thereof (0.15%)
Based on: consideration or market value, whichever HIGHER
Minimum: RM0.05 per share

EXEMPTION: Trading on Bursa Malaysia — stamp duty is typically absorbed by broker
```

### Unlisted shares (private company — Sdn Bhd)

```
Rate: Same ad valorem rates as property (1%/2%/3%/4%)
Based on: HIGHER of:
  (a) Consideration stated in the instrument
  (b) NET TANGIBLE ASSET (NTA) value per share × number of shares transferred

NTA COMPUTATION:
  Total assets (at market value, NOT book value for property)
  Less: Total liabilities
  = NTA
  NTA per share = NTA / Total issued shares
  Transfer value = NTA per share × shares transferred

CRITICAL: LHDN Stamp Office will revalue property at MARKET VALUE.
  If company owns property at book value RM1m but MV is RM5m,
  NTA is computed using RM5m. This catches many practitioners off guard.
```

## Rates — Loan / Financing Agreements

```
CONVENTIONAL LOANS:
  Rate: 0.5% (RM5 per RM1,000) of loan amount

ISLAMIC FINANCING (mudharabah, murabahah, ijarah, BBA):
  Rate: Same as conventional (0.5%)
  BUT: If the Islamic facility involves TWO instruments (e.g., property
       purchase from customer then resale under murabahah):
    → Only ONE instrument is stamped (the other is exempted under s.4(3) SA)
    → This is a common trap: paying stamp duty TWICE on Islamic facilities

REVOLVING CREDIT / OVERDRAFT:
  Rate: 0.5% of facility limit (not drawdown)
  
LETTER OF OFFER / FACILITY AGREEMENT:
  Only the PRINCIPAL instrument is dutiable
  Supporting letters/resolutions are exempt
```

## Rates — Lease / Tenancy Agreements

```
RESIDENTIAL LEASE:
  Annual rent ≤ RM2,400:     EXEMPT
  Annual rent > RM2,400:     Stamp duty as below

LEASE STAMP DUTY (based on annual rent in excess of RM2,400):
  Lease period ≤ 1 year:     RM1 per RM250 (0.4%)
  Lease period 1-3 years:    RM2 per RM250 (0.8%)
  Lease period > 3 years:    RM4 per RM250 (1.6%)
  
COMPUTATION BASE: (Monthly rent × 12) - RM2,400 = dutiable amount
  If lease includes premium/key money: add to the consideration
```

## Exemptions and Reliefs

### Section 15A — Group Relief (Stamp Act)

```
CONDITIONS (ALL must be met):
  □ Transfer between associated companies
  □ "Associated" = 90% beneficial ownership (direct or indirect)
  □ 90% relationship must exist for at least 12 months BEFORE transfer
  □ AND must be maintained for at least 24 months AFTER transfer
  □ Both companies incorporated in MALAYSIA
  □ Consideration is shares of the acquiring company (or RM100 nominal)

EFFECT: Exempt from ad valorem stamp duty. Nominal RM10 duty instead.

CLAWBACK: If 90% relationship ceases within 24 months after transfer:
  → Full stamp duty becomes payable (retrospectively)
  → LHDN enforces via stamp assessment notice

COMMON USE: Group restructuring, inter-company asset transfers, pre-IPO clean-up
```

### First-time homebuyer exemptions

```
BUDGET 2025/2026 measures (check current gazette for extension):

FIRST HOME (RM500,000 and below):
  100% stamp duty exemption on MOT
  100% stamp duty exemption on loan agreement
  Conditions: first residential property, Malaysian citizen, SPA signed within window

FIRST HOME (RM500,001 - RM1,000,000):
  75% stamp duty exemption on MOT (Budget 2025 extension)
  Conditions: same as above but higher value bracket
  
STANDARD (no exemption):
  Full ad valorem rates apply
```

### Other exemptions

```
□ Transfer between spouses [s.15(1)] — exempt
□ Transfer by way of love and affection (parent to child) — RM10 nominal
  BUT: only for DIRECT blood relative; no exemption for in-laws or siblings
□ Rescue instruments [s.15(2)] — instruments to assist company in financial difficulty
□ Instruments with no monetary consideration [s.4(3)] — RM10 nominal
□ Government instruments — exempt
□ Instruments executed by diplomatic missions — exempt
```

## Adjudication

```
WHEN TO ADJUDICATE:
  If the parties believe an instrument is exempt or attracts lower duty,
  submit to Stamp Office for ADJUDICATION (formal determination).

  Result: Stamp Office stamps "ADJUDICATED" with either:
    - "Not chargeable" (exempt)
    - "Duty of RM[X] paid" (assessed and paid)
  
  Adjudicated instrument is conclusive evidence of proper stamping.

WHEN ADJUDICATION IS MANDATORY:
  □ s.15A group relief claims
  □ s.15(1) spouse transfers
  □ Any claim for exemption where LHDN may dispute
```

## Penalties for Late Stamping

```
INSTRUMENTS MUST BE STAMPED:
  - Executed in Malaysia: within 30 days of execution
  - Executed outside Malaysia: within 30 days of receipt in Malaysia

LATE STAMPING PENALTIES:
  Stamped within 3 months of due date:    RM25 or 5% of duty (whichever greater)
  Stamped within 6 months of due date:    RM50 or 10% of duty (whichever greater)
  Stamped after 6 months from due date:   RM100 or 20% of duty (whichever greater)
  
MAXIMUM PENALTY: 20% of duty (not unlimited)

UNSTAMPED INSTRUMENT:
  → Not admissible as evidence in court [s.52 SA]
  → Can be stamped late (with penalty) to make admissible
  → LHDN may discover unstamped instruments during tax audit
```

## Stamp Duty and Tax Computation Interaction

```
FOR THE PURCHASER/TRANSFEREE:
  □ Stamp duty paid on property acquisition = INCIDENTAL COST for RPGT purposes
  □ Stamp duty paid on plant/machinery acquisition = part of qualifying expenditure for CA
  □ Stamp duty on lease = deductible revenue expense (if for business premises)

FOR THE SELLER/TRANSFEROR:
  □ Stamp duty (if borne by seller contractually) = incidental cost of disposal (RPGT)

FOR LOAN AGREEMENTS:
  □ Stamp duty on loan = not deductible for income tax purposes (capital nature)
  □ Exception: if stamp duty relates to working capital facility used in business,
    arguable as revenue deduction under s.33(1)

GROUP RESTRUCTURING:
  □ s.15A exemption + Para 38 Schedule 3 (ITA) + s.15A (Stamp Act) = common package
  □ Must satisfy BOTH Acts' conditions independently
```

## Corporate Restructuring Considerations

```
MERGER & ACQUISITION — STAMP DUTY PLANNING:

SHARE TRANSFER (buying the COMPANY):
  Stamp duty on share transfer instrument
  Rate: NTA-based (as above)
  Advantage: No MOT needed for underlying properties (company still owns them)
  Risk: If company is RPC, also triggers RPGT on shares

ASSET TRANSFER (buying ASSETS directly):
  Stamp duty on MOT for each property
  Stamp duty on instrument of transfer for each asset
  Rate: Full ad valorem on each instrument
  Disadvantage: Multiple instruments, multiple stamp duty charges
  Advantage: No RPC issue, no inherited liabilities

PLANNING:
  Often CHEAPER to buy shares (one instrument) than individual assets (multiple instruments)
  BUT: must weigh against RPGT and inherited liability risk
```

## Edge Cases and Traps

```
 1. NTA VALUATION: Stamp Office uses MARKET VALUE of property in company, not book value.
    A company with RM1 paid-up capital but RM10m property = NTA based on RM10m.
 2. ISLAMIC FINANCING: Double-stamping trap. Only ONE instrument dutiable per s.4(3).
 3. s.15A 24-MONTH RULE: If control changes within 24 months, exemption CLAWED BACK.
 4. LOVE AND AFFECTION: Only parent↔child and spouse↔spouse. Siblings NOT covered.
 5. UNLISTED SHARES: Valuation date is DATE OF INSTRUMENT, not date of payment.
 6. PART CONSIDERATION: If shares + cash as consideration, stamp on TOTAL value.
 7. FOREIGN COMPANY SHARES: If company has Malaysian property, SA may still apply.
 8. NOMINEE TRANSFERS: Transfer from nominee to beneficial owner = RM10 (adjudication needed).
 9. LEASE RENEWAL: New lease agreement = new stamp duty (not just extension of old).
10. ELECTRONIC INSTRUMENTS: e-stamping has same rules — execution date = system date.
```

---

## NEVER

- Compute stamp duty on unlisted shares using BOOK value instead of NTA (market value of assets)
- Assume s.15A applies without verifying 90% ownership for 12 months PRIOR to transfer
- Forget the 24-month post-transfer clawback on s.15A group relief
- Charge stamp duty twice on Islamic financing facilities (s.4(3) exemption for second instrument)
- Ignore that unstamped instruments are inadmissible as court evidence
- Apply first-home exemption without confirming buyer has no prior residential property
- Forget that lease stamp duty is computed on annual rent ABOVE RM2,400 (not gross)
