# NGOs, Non-Profits & Tax-Exempt Bodies

**Forms:** TF (clubs/associations/trade associations), C1 (cooperatives), C (company limited by guarantee)
**Legislative basis:** ITA 1967 — Sections 44(6), 53, 53A, 65A, 127; Schedule 6 Paragraph 12, 13
**Key Public Rulings:** PR 1/2015 (Clubs/Associations), PR 6/2005 (Trade Associations), PR 7/2024 (Cooperatives)

---

## 1. Types of Non-Profit/Exempt Organizations

### A. Section 44(6) Approved Institutions/Organizations/Funds

Bodies approved by KPHDN (Director General) for receiving tax-deductible donations. Three categories:

**Institutions:**
- Public hospitals and welfare/charity institutions
- Public universities and educational institutions
- Religious institutions
- Research organizations (serious diseases)
- Technical/vocational training centres

**Organizations:**
- Educational scholarships and financial assistance
- Religious worship and development
- Houses of worship, zoos, museums, art galleries
- Animal/environmental conservation
- ICT advancement
- Medical fundraising (serious illnesses)
- National unity promotion
- Government-partnered industrial projects

**Funds:**
- Humanitarian aid funds
- Education funds for disadvantaged students
- House of worship construction/management funds
- Educational trust funds
- School public funds

All must operate on a **non-profit basis** with clear, documented objectives.

### B. Section 127 Exempt Bodies

Minister of Finance can exempt any person/class by P.U.(A) order. Used for:
- Religious bodies (P.U.(A) 52/2017, P.U.(A) 139/2020)
- Specific categories per policy objectives

### C. Clubs, Associations, Similar Institutions (Section 53A)

Bodies formed **not for commercial purposes** — social, recreational, sports, arts, science, literature, leisure.

Examples: Athletic clubs, historical societies, residents' associations, joint management bodies, strata management corporations.

### D. Trade Associations (Section 53)

Defined under Section 53(3): association of persons/partnerships formed with main object of **safeguarding or promoting the businesses of its members**.

Conditions:
- All members must carry on a business
- Objective must be safeguarding/promoting members' businesses

Examples: Chinese Chamber of Commerce, Malaysian Association of Tours and Travel Agents, Rubber Growers Association.

A trade association is **deemed to be a body of persons** (not a partnership).

### E. Cooperatives (Section 65A)

Completely separate tax framework. Three qualifying types:
1. Registered under Co-operative Societies Act 1993
2. Incorporated under Companies Act but registered under Co-operative Societies Act
3. Farmers' Organisations (Farmers' Organisations Act 1973) and Fishermen's Associations (Fishermen's Associations Act 1971)

**Key difference:** No principle of mutuality for cooperatives. ALL income taxable (including from members).

### F. Paragraph 13, Schedule 6 Automatic Exemption

A club/association approved under subsection 44(6) **automatically** receives tax exemption on its income under Paragraph 13, Schedule 6.

Conversely: a club NOT approved under 44(6) will NOT get Paragraph 13 exemption.

## 2. Requirements for Section 44(6) Approval

### Application Process
- **Online:** Via e-Derma system through MyTax Portal
- **Manual:** Direct submission to KPHDN
- **Prerequisite:** Two-year operational history required

### Constitutional Requirements
1. **Purpose and Beneficiaries**: clearly stated
2. **Objectives**: clear intentions and aspirations
3. **Governance/Decision-Making**: general meetings, elections, authority limits, procedures
4. **Membership**: eligibility, duties/rights, admission/termination
5. **Financial Controls**: documented internal controls
6. **Accounting and Audit**: designated periods and processes
7. **Dissolution Provisions**: follow-up actions, financial responsibility
8. **Amendment Protocol**: post-approval changes require DG written approval BEFORE submission to other bodies

### Conditions to Maintain Exemption

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| 50% Spending Rule | Must spend ≥ 50% of prior-year income annually on charitable activities |
| What counts | Salaries supporting charitable work count; depreciation, bad debts, asset purchases do NOT |
| Business Activity Cap | Maximum 25% of accumulated funds for business activities |
| Board Composition | > 50% of board must be external (non-affiliated) parties |
| Remuneration Restriction | Founders, family, employed staff cannot receive salaries — only expense reimbursement |
| Approval Renewal | Extension request 6 months before expiry |
| Constitutional Changes | Written DG approval required before submitting to other regulators |

### Revocation
If objective is contravened, approved status withdrawn:
- Gifts of money received become taxable
- Donors lose their deduction

## 3. When is an NGO Taxable?

### Clubs/Associations (Section 53A, from YA 2009)

| Income Type | Taxable? | Basis |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Members' subscription fees | No | Mutual receipt (s.53A(2)) |
| Members' entrance fees | No | Mutual receipt |
| Food/drinks sold to members | No | Mutual receipt |
| Members' dinner/function tickets | No | Mutual receipt |
| Non-member dinner tickets | **Yes** | Non-mutual |
| Fundraising from public | **Yes** | Non-mutual |
| Drinks sold to non-members | **Yes** | Non-mutual |
| Hall/facility hire to public | **Yes** | Non-mutual |
| Interest from deposits | **Yes** | Investment income |
| Rental from tenants | **Yes** | Investment/rental income |
| Dividends | **Yes** | Investment income |
| Raffle/lucky draw (non-members) | **Yes** | Non-mutual |
| Transactions with members' spouse/parents/children | **Yes** | Treated as non-members |

### Trade Associations (Section 53(3))
ALL revenue receipts form **deemed business income** — subscriptions, entrance fees, seminar fees, trade activities, contributions.

Exception: Building fund donations = capital receipts (not taxable).

### Cooperatives
NO mutuality principle. ALL income taxable. Subject to specific exemptions under Schedule 6.

## 4. Tax Computation

### Clubs/Associations

1. Identify gross non-mutual receipts (taxable)
2. Deduct allowable expenses attributable to non-member transactions
3. Apportion common expenses: **A × B/C**
   - A = total outgoings/expenses
   - B = gross income from non-members
   - C = total gross income (members + non-members)
4. Capital allowances also apportioned: **D × E/F**
5. Add other income (interest, rent, dividends)
6. Deduct current year losses
7. Tax at **individual scale rates** (Part I, Schedule 1) — NOT corporate rates

**Gift of money deduction (s.44(6)):**
- Formula: G × H/I (apportioned to non-member income)
- Cap: shall not exceed **7% of aggregate income** for the basis period

### Trade Associations

1. Net profit per accounts
2. Remove interest income (separate source)
3. Add back non-deductible items
4. = Adjusted income from deemed business
5. Less capital allowances
6. = Statutory income from business
7. Add statutory income from interest
8. Tax at **individual scale rates** (resident) or **28% flat** (non-resident)

### Loss Rules for Clubs/Associations
- Losses from member transactions CANNOT offset non-member income
- Such losses are disregarded
- Losses from non-member transactions can be carried forward against future non-member income

## 5. Cooperative-Specific Tax Treatment

### Schedule 6, Paragraph 12 Exemptions

**First 5 years:** Any newly registered cooperative — exempt from tax on ALL income.

**After 5 years:** Exempt ONLY if members' funds at first day of basis period < **RM750,000**.

**Members' funds =**
- Paid-up capital (shares and subscriptions)
- Statutory reserve fund
- Other reserves (excluding capital reserve from revaluation and depreciation provisions)
- Share premium account balance
- Profit and loss appropriation account balance
- **Excludes:** members' savings accounts, members' loan accounts, overdrafts, preference shares

### Special Deductions (Section 65A)
- **s.65A(a):** Amounts to statutory reserve fund, educational institutions, Co-operative Education/Development Trust Fund — capped at **25% of audited net profits**
- **s.65A(b):** **8% of members' funds** at first day of basis period

### Cooperative Tax Rates (Part IV, Schedule 1)

| Chargeable Income (RM) | Rate |
|------------------------|------|
| First 30,000 | 0% |
| 30,001 - 60,000 | 5% |
| 60,001 - 100,000 | 10% |
| 100,001 - 150,000 | 15% |
| 150,001 - 250,000 | 18% |
| 250,001 - 500,000 | 21% |
| 500,001 - 750,000 | 23% |
| Over 750,000 | 24% |

## 6. Trade Association Exemption Orders

### Income Tax (Exemption) (No. 8) Order 2002
- Resident trade associations established 1/1/1996 to 31/12/2001
- Exemption: 50% of statutory income from all sources for maximum 5 years

### Income Tax (Exemption) (No. 19) Order 2005
- All resident trade associations, from YA 2005
- Exemption: statutory income derived from **members' subscription fees**
- Exempt statutory income = gross subscription fees less expenses and Schedule 3 allowances attributable
- Common expenses apportioned: A × B/C
- Cannot be claimed in same YA as No. 8 Order

| Entity Type | Form | Filing Method |
|-------------|------|---------------|
| Club/Association/Similar Institution | Form TF | e-Filing or manual |
| Trade Association (Societies Act) | Form TF | Tax file starts with "F" |
| Trade Association (company limited by guarantee) | Form TF | Must get "F" number |
| Cooperative Society | Form C1 | e-Filing mandatory from YA 2024 |

### Deadlines
- Form TF/C1: **7 months after accounting period closes**
- For clubs with basis year = calendar year: due ~30 April [VERIFY against RF Filing Programme]
- ALL non-profit organizations must file returns even if exempt or operating at a loss

### Record Keeping
- **7 years** from end of YA in which return is filed
- Must distinguish between member and non-member transactions

## 8. Donor Tax Deductions

### Section 44(6) Deduction
- **Cash donations only** — in-kind NOT eligible for tax deduction receipts
- Cap: **10% of taxpayer's aggregate income** (individuals and companies)
- Government donations (Kew-38 receipt): fully deductible, no cap

### Section 44(11A) — Company Zakat
- Zakat paid by companies to approved bodies: deductible up to **2.5% of aggregate income**

### Section 44(11D) — Waqf/Endowment
- Separate provisions for waqf funds
- Waqf management corporations under State Government Enactment qualify
- Over 50% of committee must be external parties

### Section 44(8) — Library Facilities
- Cash donations for library facilities deductible

### Section 44(11) — Gift of Paintings
- Gifts of paintings deductible under separate provision

### Zakat vs Section 44(6) — Individuals
- Zakat: **tax rebate** under Section 6A(3) — dollar-for-dollar reduction, limited to tax charged
- Section 44(6): **deduction from aggregate income** — reduces taxable income before rate application
- Separate provisions — can claim both

## 10. Common Entity Types in Practice

### Yayasan (Foundations)
- Established under state/federal enactments or as companies limited by guarantee
- Apply for Section 44(6) approval
- Subject to 50% spending and 25% business activity rules
- Majority external board members required

### Persatuan (Associations/Societies)
- Registered under Societies Act 1966 with ROS
- Taxed under Section 53A
- Mutual receipts non-taxable; non-member income taxable
- File Form TF with "F" tax file number

### Badan Kebajikan (Welfare Bodies)
- Can apply for Section 44(6) approval
- Subject to same conditions
- Must demonstrate 50% charitable expenditure threshold

### Religious Bodies (Masjid, Churches, Temples)
- Specific exemption orders: P.U.(A) 52/2017, P.U.(A) 139/2020
- Dedicated LHDN guidelines for "Places of Worship"
- Can apply for Section 44(6) approval
- Waqf funds under Section 44(11D) for Islamic bodies

### Political Parties [UNCERTAINTY]
- Registered under Societies Act 1966
- Generally NOT approved under Section 44(6) — donations NOT tax-deductible
- Mutual income (subscriptions) non-taxable under mutuality
- Non-member activity income taxable
