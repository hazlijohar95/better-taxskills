# Sdn Bhd (Private Limited Company) - Corporate Tax Rules

**Form:** C
**Legislative basis:** Income Tax Act 1967
**Filing deadline:** 7 months from date of closing of accounts
**Tax authority:** Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri (LHDN)
**Effective:** YA 2025/2026 (includes Budget 2026 measures tabled 10 October 2025)

---

## 1. Tax Rates

### Standard Rate (YA 2024 onwards)
- Resident company: **24%**
- Non-resident company: **24%**

### SME Preferential Rate (YA 2025)

| Chargeable Income Band | Tax Rate |
|------------------------|----------|
| First RM150,000 | 15% |
| RM150,001 - RM600,000 | 17% |
| Above RM600,000 | 24% |

### SME Qualifying Conditions (ALL must be met)

| Condition | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Paid-up capital of ordinary shares | ≤ RM2.5 million |
| Gross income from business | ≤ RM50 million |
| Does not control another company with paid-up capital > RM2.5 million | Yes |
| Not controlled by another company with paid-up capital > RM2.5 million | Yes |
| Foreign ownership | ≤ 20% of paid-up capital (direct or indirect) |

### Global Minimum Tax (YA 2025 onwards)
- Multinational Top-Up Tax (MTT): applies to Malaysian parent entities of MNE groups where effective rate < 15%
- Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT): 15% for Malaysian operations of qualifying MNE groups
- Financial years commencing on or after 1 January 2025
- UTPR: NOT adopted

## 2. Basis Period and Year of Assessment

**Section 21A, ITA 1967**

- Current-year basis assessment
- A company is taxed on income from all sources arising in its financial year ending in the calendar year that coincides with that YA
- Example: FYE 30 June 2025 → assessed for YA 2025

## 3. Tax Computation Structure

```
FOR EACH SOURCE (Section 4):

1.  Gross Income (Section 22)
2.  Less: Allowable Expenses (Section 33(1))
3.  Less: Specific Deductions (Section 34)
4.  Add back: Disallowed Expenses (Section 39)
    = ADJUSTED INCOME / (ADJUSTED LOSS) per source

5.  Less: Capital Allowances (Schedule 3)
    = STATUTORY INCOME per source
    (Cannot be less than nil per source)

AGGREGATE ALL SOURCES:
6.  Total Statutory Income (all sources)
7.  Less: Approved Donations (capped at 10% of aggregate income) [Section 44(6)]
    = AGGREGATE INCOME

8.  Less: Current year business loss set-off [Section 44(2)]
9.  Less: Brought forward business losses [Section 44(5A)/(5B)]
10. Less: Prospecting expenditure, pre-operational business expenditure
    = TOTAL INCOME

11. Less: Tax-exempt income (Pioneer status exemption, etc.)
    = CHARGEABLE INCOME

12. Tax on Chargeable Income at applicable rate
13. Less: Tax rebates [Section 110 bilateral credit, Section 132 unilateral credit]
14. Less: Tax deducted at source [Section 110]
15. Less: Monthly tax instalments paid (CP204)
    = TAX PAYABLE / (TAX REFUNDABLE)
```

## 4. Classes of Income (Section 4)

| Section | Class | Description |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| 4(a) | Business | Gains/profits from a business, trade, profession, or vocation |
| 4(b) | Employment | Gains/profits from employment |
| 4(c) | Dividends, interest, discounts | Investment income |
| 4(d) | Rents, royalties, premiums | Letting of property, IP royalties |
| 4(e) | Pensions, annuities, periodic payments | Periodic receipts |
| 4(f) | Other gains or profits | Catch-all |

Key notes for Sdn Bhd:
- Section 4(a) business income is the primary source for trading companies
- Dividends from resident companies are exempt under single-tier system (Section 12(1B))
- Foreign-sourced income received in Malaysia is taxable (from YA 2022), subject to transitional provisions

## 5. Adjusted Income Computation (Section 33)

### The General Deduction Test - Section 33(1)

Deductible: all outgoings and expenses **wholly and exclusively** incurred during the basis period in the production of gross income from that source.

Four conditions (all must be met):
1. Must be actual expenditure (not provisions/reserves)
2. Wholly and exclusively for business
3. Incurred during the basis period
4. Direct nexus to income production from that source

### Specifically Enumerated Deductions

| Section | Item |
|---------|------|
| 33(1)(a) | Interest on borrowed capital employed in income production |
| 33(1)(b) | Rent for land/buildings used for income production |
| 33(1)(c) | Repairs to premises, plant, machinery, fixtures |
| 33(1)(d) | Prescribed deductions (gateway for subsidiary legislation) |

## 6. Disallowed Expenses (Section 39)

| Section | Item |
|---------|------|
| 39(1)(a) | Domestic or private expenses |
| 39(1)(b) | Capital expenditure |
| 39(1)(c) | Depreciation of assets |
| 39(1)(d) | Expenses not wholly and exclusively for income production |
| 39(1)(e) | Income tax or similar taxes |
| 39(1)(f) | Contributions to unapproved schemes |
| 39(1)(g) | Employer's own pension/retirement |
| 39(1)(h) | Leave passage exceeding limits |
| 39(1)(l) | Entertainment (50% disallowance as general rule) |
| 39(1)(m) | General provisions (only specific bad debts allowed) |

### Entertainment - Section 39(1)(l)
- General rule: 50% deductible only
- 100% deductible exceptions:
  - Staff entertainment (annual dinners, team events)
  - Promotional activities (trade fairs, product launches)
  - Entertainment incidental to employee entertainment
  - Food/beverage/entertainment companies (as part of business)
  - Promotional gifts with company branding (≤ RM500/unit)

### Provisions and Bad Debts
- General provisions: NOT deductible
- Specific bad debts (identified, reasonably irrecoverable): deductible
- Recovered bad debts previously allowed: taxable in year of recovery

### Other Common Disallowances
- Penalties, fines, compound payments
- Donations to non-approved bodies
- Political contributions
- Lease rental on non-commercial vehicles above RM50,000 (vehicles ≤ RM150,000) or RM100,000 (vehicles > RM150,000)

## 8. Loss Rules

### Current Year Loss - Section 44(2)
- Business losses from any source can be set off against income from ALL sources in the same YA
- Includes set-off against non-business income (interest, rental)

### Brought Forward Losses - Section 44(5A)/(5B) (from YA 2019)
- Maximum carry-forward: **10 consecutive years**
- Can ONLY be set off against **business source income** (not non-business)
- Clock starts from YA the loss arises

### Unabsorbed Capital Allowances
- Carry forward **indefinitely**
- Can only be utilised against income from the **same underlying business source**

### No Carry-Back
- No provisions to carry losses back to prior YAs

### Group Relief - Section 44A

**Conditions (both companies must satisfy):**
1. Resident and incorporated in Malaysia
2. Paid-up capital > RM2.5 million at beginning of basis period
3. Same 12-month accounting period
4. "Related" throughout basis period AND preceding 12 months
5. Not enjoying PS, ITA, RA, or similar incentives
6. No unutilised ITA or unabsorbed pioneer losses post-incentive

**"Related" definition:**
- Either company owns ≥ 70% of ordinary shares of the other, OR a third company owns ≥ 70% of each
- AND: entitled to ≥ 70% of distributable profits and assets on winding up

**Limits:**
- Maximum surrender: 70% of adjusted loss for that YA
- Limited to first 3 consecutive YAs following commencement
- Irrevocable election in tax return

## 9. Estimate of Tax Payable - CP204

### Filing
- Must be furnished no later than **30 days before the beginning of the basis period**
- Example: FYE 31 Dec 2025 → CP204 due by 1 December 2024

### New Company Exemption
- Paid-up capital ≤ RM2.5 million: exempt from CP204 for **2 years** from YA of commencement

### Revision of Estimates (CP204A)
- 6th month: can increase or decrease
- 9th month: can only increase (no downward revision)

### Monthly Instalments
- Payable in monthly instalments from the 2nd month of basis period
- Total estimate ÷ 12

### Short Basis Period
- Not required if basis period < 6 months

### Underestimation Penalty - Section 107C(9)/(10)
- If (Actual Tax - Estimated Tax) > 30% of Actual Tax:
  - Penalty = 10% × (shortfall beyond 30% threshold)

## 10. Filing - Form C

- Due: **within 7 months** from closing of accounts
- Example: FYE 31 Dec 2025 → due 31 July 2026
- Tax return is deemed a notice of assessment
- Balance of tax due on same deadline
- E-filing available

---

## 11. Penalties

| Offence | Penalty |
|---------|---------|
| Late filing (Section 112(3)) | 20%-35% of tax payable |
| Prosecution for failure to file | RM200-RM20,000 fine or ≤ 6 months imprisonment |
| Incorrect return (Section 113) | Penalty ≤ amount of tax undercharged (100%) |
| Late payment | 10% increase; further 5% if unpaid after 60 days |
| CP204 underestimation | 10% on excess beyond 30% threshold |
| Failure to furnish CP204 | RM200-RM20,000 fine or ≤ 6 months imprisonment |
| Wilful evasion (Section 114) | RM1,000-RM20,000 fine or ≤ 3 years imprisonment + 300% special penalty |
| Late WHT remittance | 10% penalty on amount not remitted |
| Statute of limitations | 5 years (no limit for fraud/wilful default/negligence) |

## 12. Foreign-Sourced Income (from YA 2022)

- Foreign-sourced income received in Malaysia by resident companies is taxable (from YA 2022)
- Tax exemption on foreign-sourced dividends and capital gains for resident companies and LLPs: **extended to 31 Dec 2030** (Budget 2026)
- Scope expanded to include cooperative societies and trust bodies
- Prior transitional 3% concessionary rate applied for certain FSI periods

---

## 13. Dividend Tax (from YA 2025)

- **2% tax** on dividend income received by individual shareholders exceeding **RM100,000 per annum**
- Effective from YA 2025
- Single-tier system remains (no tax at company level on distribution)

---

## 14. LLP Profit Distribution Tax (NEW YA 2026)

- **2% tax** on profit distributions from LLPs exceeding **RM100,000 per annum** to individual partners
- Applies to resident and non-resident individual partners
- Purpose: align LLP income treatment with company dividend tax
- Effective: YA 2026

---

## 15. Transfer Pricing - Section 140A

- DGIR empowered to adjust transactions between associated persons not at arm's length
- Acceptable methods: CUP, Resale Price, Cost Plus, TNMM, Profit Split
- Documentation requirements: contemporaneous TP documentation
- Penalty: 5% surcharge on TP adjustments
- APA available (unilateral, bilateral, multilateral)

### Country-by-Country Reporting
- Applies to MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ RM3 billion
- Due within 12 months after close of FY

### Earnings Stripping Rules - Section 140C
- Replaces thin capitalisation
- Applies to interest expense > RM500,000 in controlled cross-border transactions
- Maximum deduction: 20% of tax-EBITDA per business source
- Excess carried forward indefinitely (subject to shareholders' continuity)
