# Sole Proprietors - Tax Rules (Form B)

**Form:** B
**Legislative basis:** Income Tax Act 1967
**Key Public Rulings:** PR 1/2005 (Self-Assessment)
**Filing deadline:** 30 June of the following year (with business income)
**Advance tax:** CP500

---

## 1. Tax Treatment

Business income is taxed as one source under Section 4(a). The same Section 33/39 rules for adjusted income apply as for companies. The key difference: sole proprietor's business income flows into the individual tax computation and is subject to progressive rates after personal reliefs.

## 5. Capital Allowances

Same Schedule 3 rules apply as for companies:

| Asset | IA (%) | AA (%) |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Heavy machinery & motor vehicles | 20 | 20 |
| General plant and machinery | 20 | 14 |
| Office equipment / furniture | 20 | 10 |
| ICT equipment and software | 40 | 20 |
| Small value assets (< RM2,000 each) | — | 100 |

**Budget 2026 ACA (11 Oct 2025 - 31 Dec 2026):**
- ICT equipment and computer software: **20% IA / 40% AA** (accelerated from standard)
- Heavy/general machinery from local manufacturers: **20% IA / 40% AA** (accelerated)

Motor vehicle QE cap: RM50,000 general; RM100,000 for new vehicle costing ≤ RM150,000. No EV-specific cap has been enacted.

Small value asset annual cap: RM20,000 (no cap if qualifying as SME equivalent — verify applicability to sole proprietors).

## 6. Business Losses

### Current Year - Section 44(2)
Business loss can be set off against ALL income sources in the same YA (rental, interest, employment, etc.)

### Carried Forward
- Maximum 10 consecutive years (from YA 2019)
- Can only offset against statutory income from business sources

### Key Difference from Sdn Bhd
Sole proprietor's business loss can offset against employment income or rental income in the same year. This personal offset is a significant advantage over incorporation in loss-making years.

---

## 7. Advance Tax - CP500 (Section 107B)

### Who Receives CP500
LHDN issues CP500 to individuals with business income, based on the preceding year's tax liability.

### Payment Schedule
- Bi-monthly instalments (6 instalments per year)
- Starting from March of the YA
- Amount determined by LHDN based on prior year

### Revision
- Can apply to revise CP500 by the 30th of June (before the 3rd instalment)

### Penalty
- If actual tax exceeds CP500 estimate by > 30%: penalty of 10% on excess beyond 30%

---

## 8. Filing - Form B

- Deadline: **30 June** of the following year (for individuals with business income)
- E-filing available and encouraged
- Balance of tax payable by same deadline

---

## 9. Key Differences from Sdn Bhd

| Aspect | Sole Proprietor | Sdn Bhd |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| Tax rate | Progressive 0%-30% | 15%/17%/24% (SME) or flat 24% |
| Personal reliefs | Available | Not available |
| Loss offset | Against all personal income | Against business income only (B/F) |
| Filing deadline | 30 June | 7 months from FYE |
| Advance tax | CP500 (LHDN-determined) | CP204 (self-estimated) |
| Capital allowances | Same rules apply | Same rules apply |
| Deduction rules (S.33/39) | Identical | Identical |
| Separate legal entity | No | Yes |
| Liability | Unlimited personal | Limited to company assets |
| Zakat | Tax rebate (dollar-for-dollar) | Deduction from aggregate income (≤2.5%) |

---

## 10. SSM Registration

- Sole proprietors register under the **Registration of Businesses Act 1956** with SSM
- Business name registration required
- Annual renewal required
- Not the same as Companies Act 2016 incorporation (which is for Sdn Bhd)

---

## 11. Zakat Treatment (Muslim Individuals)

- **Section 6A(3):** Zakat paid to approved religious authorities is a **tax rebate** (not a deduction)
- Dollar-for-dollar reduction of tax charged
- Rebate limited to the amount of tax charged for that YA
- Separate from Section 44(6) donation deductions — can claim both
