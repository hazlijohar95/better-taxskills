# Capital Allowances - Schedule 3, ITA 1967

**Legislative Reference:** Schedule 3, Income Tax Act 1967 [Section 42]
**Key Public Rulings:** PR 1/2001, PR 2/2001, PR 1/2005, PR 1/2008, PR 4/2013, PR 04/2016, PR 07/2018, PR 10/2020, KU 03/2021
**Key Subsidiary Legislation:** P.U.(A) 52/2000 (Annual Allowance Rates)
**Last updated:** Includes Budget 2026 ACA measures + gazetted e-Invoice ACA (April 2026)

---

## 1. Qualifying Expenditure (QE)

### 1.1 Qualifying Plant Expenditure (Paragraph 2)

Capital expenditure incurred on provision of machinery or plant used for business, including:

**(a)** Alteration of existing building for installing machinery/plant, and incidental installation costs [Para 2(1)(a)]

**(b)** Preparing, cutting, tunnelling, levelling land for installation — BUT only if ≤ 10% of aggregate of site preparation cost + machinery cost [Para 2(1)(b)]

**(c)** Fish ponds, animal pens, chicken houses, cages, buildings (excluding director/management living accommodation), structures for poultry/livestock/agriculture [Para 2(1)(c)]

### 1.2 Exclusions from QE

- Land cost — never qualifying
- Living accommodation for directors, controllers, management/admin/clerical staff
- Expenditure deductible under Chapter 4 Part III (revenue deductions) [Para 54]
- Non-business assets
- Non-resident service fees on installation if WHT not deducted [Para 2D]
- GST input tax where person failed to register or could have claimed credit [Para 2E]

### 1.3 Motor Vehicle QE Cap (Paragraph 2(2))

For non-commercial motor vehicles (not licensed for commercial transport):

| Condition | QE Cap |
|-----------|--------|
| General rule (from YA 1991) | **RM50,000** |
| New vehicle, total cost ≤ RM150,000, purchased on/after 28.10.2000 | **RM100,000** |
| Licensed for commercial transport of goods/passengers | **No cap** (full cost) |

Conditions for RM100,000:
1. Vehicle NOT previously used (brand new)
2. Total cost ≤ RM150,000
3. Purchased on/after 28 October 2000

If any condition fails → reverts to RM50,000.

### 1.4 Second-Hand / Used Assets

**Para 2A (non-business to business):** QE = market value at date brought into business use. No initial allowance.

**Para 2C (overseas to Malaysia):** QE = market value at date brought into use in Malaysia. No initial allowance.

### 1.5 Hire Purchase Assets (Paragraph 46)

- Person deemed to be owner for Schedule 3 purposes
- QE in any basis period = capital portion of HP instalments paid in that period (excluding interest)
- IA and AA computed on cumulative capital portion paid
- Small value asset rules (Para 19A) do NOT apply to HP assets [PR 1/2008 Para 5]

### 1.6 Cost Determination (Paragraph 55)

- Construction of building: day construction completed
- Provision of machinery/plant: day first used for business
- Other expenditure: day amount becomes payable
- Pre-business expenditure: deemed incurred on first day of business

### 1.7 Qualifying Building Expenditure (Paragraph 3)

Capital expenditure on construction or purchase of industrial building. For purchases: QE = purchase price including legal fees, stamp duty, incidental costs, EXCLUDING land cost.

### 1.8 Site Preparation 75% Rule (Paragraph 67)

If site preparation cost exceeds 75% of aggregate (site preparation + machinery cost) → machinery deemed not to exist → no CA.

## 2. Initial Allowance (IA)

### Definition
One-time allowance given in the year asset is first used for business. [Para 9]

### Rates

| Asset Category | IA Rate | Reference |
|----------------|---------|-----------|
| ALL plant and machinery | **20%** | Para 10 |
| Industrial buildings | **10%** | Para 12 |

IA is uniformly 20% for all plant/machinery. Differentiation occurs at AA stage.

### Conditions (Paragraph 13)

**Plant/machinery [Para 13(a)]:** No IA if at end of basis period:
- Person was NOT owner, OR
- Asset NOT in use for business
- Exception: if disposed during period, IA allowed if asset in use within 1 month before disposal

**Buildings [Para 13(c)]:** No IA if at end of basis period:
- Person NOT owner, OR
- Not in use/about to be used as industrial building

**No IA for second-hand/non-business assets** [Para 13A]: Assets under Para 2A, 2C get no IA (AA only).

## 3. Annual Allowance (AA)

### Definition
Given for each YA during which person owns the asset and it is in use for business at end of basis period. [Para 14]

## 4. Small Value Assets (Paragraph 19A)

### Threshold and Cap

| Period | Threshold/Asset | Annual Cap | Reference |
|--------|-----------------|------------|-----------|
| YA 2006-2014 | RM1,000 | RM10,000/YA | PR 1/2008 |
| YA 2015 onwards | **RM2,000** | **RM20,000/YA** | KU 03/2021 |

Allowance = **100% of QE** in year of acquisition (immediate write-off).

### Conditions
- Must be owned at end of basis period
- Must be in use for business at end of basis period
- Expected life span > 2 years
- Does NOT apply to HP assets [PR 1/2008 Para 5]
- Person may elect either Para 19A or normal CA — election must be consistent per asset

### SME vs Non-SME

**Non-SME:** Annual cap of RM20,000 applies. Excess claimed under normal rates.

**SME:** Annual cap does NOT apply. Unlimited small value asset claims. [Para 19A(3)]

**SME Definition for Para 19A:**
- Resident and incorporated in Malaysia
- Paid-up capital (ordinary shares) ≤ RM2,500,000 at beginning of basis period
- Gross income from all business sources ≤ RM50,000,000

**Exclusions from SME status [Para 19A(4)]:**
Company does NOT qualify if > 50% of ordinary share capital directly/indirectly owned by a related company (paid-up capital > RM2,500,000).

### Disposal
If disposed → balancing charge arises (disposal value > residual expenditure of zero).

## 6. Industrial Building Allowance (IBA)

### What Qualifies (Paragraphs 63-66)

**Industrial building (Para 63):**
- (a) Factory (Para 64 definition)
- (b) Dock, wharf, jetty
- (c) Warehouse used for storage hire
- (d) Water/electricity/telecom/hydraulic undertaking
- (e) Buildings for agriculture/mining/fishing purposes
- (f) Mine/quarry buildings

**Factory definition (Para 64):** A building in which articles are manufactured, processed, or assembled; goods are altered, repaired, ornamented, finished, adapted for sale, or broken up; materials are subjected to any process. Includes research laboratory attached to manufacturing.

### 10% De Minimis Rule (Paragraph 66)
Where part of building used as office/dwelling ≤ 10% of total floor area → entire building treated as industrial building. If > 10%, only the industrial portion qualifies.

### Buildings Treated as Industrial Buildings
- Para 37A: Private hospital/maternity/nursing home
- Para 37B: R&D building
- Para 37C: Export goods storage/imported goods processing warehouse
- Para 37E: Approved service project building (Schedule 7B)
- Para 37F: Hotel (registered with MOT)
- Para 37G: Airport building
- Para 37H: Motor racing circuit
- Para 42A: Employee living accommodation (manufacturing/hotel/tourism/approved service)
- Para 42B: School/educational institution (approved)
- Para 42C: Industrial/technical/vocational training

## 7. Balancing Adjustments (Paragraphs 34-37)

### When They Arise
Upon disposal, sale, or cessation of use for business.

### Balancing Allowance
- Disposal value < Residual expenditure
- BA = Residual expenditure − Disposal value
- Further deduction in year of disposal

### Balancing Charge
- Disposal value > Residual expenditure
- BC = Disposal value − Residual expenditure
- Treated as income in year of disposal
- **BC capped at total allowances previously claimed** [Para 37]

### Calculation
```
Residual Expenditure = QE − Total IA and AA claimed to date

If Disposal Price > Residual Expenditure → Balancing Charge (capped)
If Disposal Price < Residual Expenditure → Balancing Allowance
```

### Motor Vehicle Proportioning (Paragraph 62)
Where motor vehicle QE was capped (e.g., actual cost RM200,000, QE capped at RM100,000), disposal value for BA/BC purposes is proportioned:

Disposal value for tax = Actual disposal price × (Capped QE / Actual cost)

### Gift to Training Institutes
Disposal value = zero (no balancing charge).

## 8. Special Rules

### 2-Year Ownership Claw-Back (Paragraph 71)
[VERIFY: specific conditions under which CA is clawed back if asset disposed within 2 years]

### Partly Business Use (Paragraph 73)
Where asset used partly for business and partly not, CA is apportioned based on extent of business use.

### Controlled Transfers (Paragraphs 38-40)
Transfers between related parties — special rules to prevent manipulation of balancing adjustments.

### Shareholder Change Rule (Paragraphs 75A-75C)
Unabsorbed CA may be lost if substantial change in shareholders occurs (continuity test).

### Temporarily Disused Assets (Paragraphs 56-57)
- Asset temporarily not in use: AA still allowed if cessation is temporary and due to circumstances
- Asset permanently ceased: no further AA from that point

### Assets Held for Sale (Paragraph 61A)
Where asset held for sale at end of basis period — specific treatment applies.

### Dismantling Costs (Paragraph 67C)
Capital expenditure on dismantling plant/machinery previously used for business may be QE.

### Definition of "Plant" (Paragraph 70A)
Includes apparatus, equipment, or machinery used for carrying on a business but does NOT include a building or structure. Exception for buildings qualifying under specific paragraphs.
