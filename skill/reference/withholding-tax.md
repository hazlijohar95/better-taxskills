# Withholding Tax Obligations

**Legislative basis:** ITA 1967, Sections 107A, 109, 109B, 109C, 109D, 109F
**Remittance deadline:** Within 1 month from date of payment/crediting to non-resident
**Penalty for late remittance:** 10% on WHT amount not remitted on time

---

## 1. Overview

Withholding tax applies to payments made to non-residents. The payer (Malaysian company/person) must deduct tax at source and remit to LHDN.

---

## 2. WHT Rates (Non-Treaty)

| Payment Type | Section | WHT Rate | Notes |
|-------------|---------|----------|-------|
| Dividends | — | **0%** | Single-tier system |
| Interest | 109 | **15%** | To non-resident |
| Royalties | 109 | **10%** | To non-resident |
| Technical fees | 109B (s.4A) | **10%** | Technical/management services |
| Management fees | 109B (s.4A) | **10%** | |
| Rental of movable property | 109B (s.4A) | **10%** | To non-resident |
| Services rendered in Malaysia | 109B (s.4A) | **10%** | By non-resident |
| Contract payments | 107A | **13%** | (10% contractor + 3% employees) |
| Other income to non-residents | — | **10%** | Catch-all |
| Public entertainer | 109A | **15%** | |

---

## 3. Section 4A — Special Classes of Income

Payments subject to WHT under Section 109B include amounts paid to non-residents for:
- Technical or management fees
- Rental of movable property
- Payments for services rendered in Malaysia
- Advice, assistance, or services in connection with technical management or administration

---

## 4. Contract Payments (Section 107A)

### Rate
- **13%** deduction at source:
  - 10% on account of contractor's tax liability
  - 3% on account of employees' tax liability

### Scope
Applies to contract payments to non-resident contractors for services rendered in Malaysia.

### Nature
This is NOT a final tax — it is a withholding on account. The actual tax liability is determined upon filing.

---

## 5. Double Taxation Agreements (DTAs)

WHT rates may be reduced under Malaysia's network of DTAs. The payer must verify:
1. Whether a DTA exists with the recipient's country of residence
2. The treaty rate for the specific income type
3. That the recipient qualifies for treaty benefits (beneficial ownership, LOB clauses)

Common treaty rates are lower than domestic rates (e.g., interest may be reduced to 10%, royalties to 8%, technical fees may be exempt or reduced).

---

## 6. Timing and Compliance

### Remittance Deadline
- Within **1 month** from the date of payment/crediting to non-resident

### Penalty
- **10% penalty** on WHT amount not remitted within prescribed period

### Consequence for Payer's Deduction
- If payer fails to deduct/remit WHT: the expense becomes **non-deductible** until WHT is paid [Section 39(2)/(3)]
- This effectively makes WHT compliance a condition for tax deductibility

---

## 7. WHT and Deductibility Interaction

| Scenario | Expense Deductible? |
|----------|-------------------|
| WHT deducted and remitted on time | Yes |
| WHT deducted but remitted late | Yes (but 10% penalty on WHT) |
| WHT not deducted | No — disallowed until WHT paid |
| WHT not applicable (domestic payment) | Yes (normal rules) |

---

## 8. Exemptions and Exceptions

### Interest — Resident Recipients
- Generally 0% WHT on interest paid to residents
- Exception: 5% on certain interest to resident individuals in specific circumstances

### Single-Tier Dividends
- 0% WHT on dividends (single-tier system eliminates dividend WHT)

### Exempt Under Treaty
- Certain income types may be exempt from WHT under specific DTAs
- Must have valid Certificate of Residence from recipient's tax authority

---

## 9. Practical Compliance Steps

1. Identify payment to non-resident
2. Classify payment type (interest, royalty, technical fee, contract, etc.)
3. Check if DTA exists and applies
4. Apply correct WHT rate (treaty or domestic)
5. Deduct WHT from payment
6. Remit to LHDN within 1 month using prescribed form
7. Issue withholding tax certificate to non-resident recipient
8. Retain records for 7 years
